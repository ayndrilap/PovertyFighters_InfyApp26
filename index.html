The Carezy Ecosystem

Our streamlined, four-pillar framework built to provide a direct path to stability:

Immediate Relief

Consolidates verified local distribution centers for free clothing and clothing drives, removing the guesswork for users in transitional phases.

Nutritional Support

Connects individuals to community kitchens and food pantries with real-time hours, ensuring access to hot meals without geographic barriers.

Emergency Housing

Maps out nearby safe shelters and temporary housing networks, filtering by immediate vacancy to provide crucial overnight security.

Economic Pathways

Aggregates entry-level job networks and community mentorship programs, helping users transition from immediate relief to long-term stability.

The Pitch

Poverty and job insecurities deeply affect many NYC communities and residents. Carezy is designed to change that by offering immediate, streamlined relief. Our mission is to meet our users where they are, providing clear resources to offer relief directly to individuals facing homeless, food, or job insecurities.

Why We Built This

We wanted to help our peers and neighbors navigate out of vulnerable situations towards systemic stability. Through extensive research, we realized that people in tight schedules or living far from resources need an immediate, dependable tool—which drove us to create Carezy as an all-in-one path to local support networks.
